Renewal list for October - and the lapsed list

Every month I send renewal letters to members whose membership runs out the following month, and a
'we miss you' letter to members who have lapsed. It's the 15th of September, so this round is
October renewals.

A few rules, because the export does not know them:

1. Grace period. Members keep their benefits for a while after their membership runs out, and they
   are still members during that time. Annual members get 30 days after their expiration date;
   monthly sustainers get 10 days after the date they are paid through. Count the grace days from
   the day after: a membership that expires on 1 August is in grace through 31 August and lapsed from
   1 September. Anyone still in their grace period goes on neither list - the front desk handles them.
   The Status column in the export says Expired the day after expiry, so please don't go by it.

2. Monthly sustainers are charged every month on the same day, so each charge pays them through the
   same date the next month. The system shows them Active forever and leaves Expiration blank - go by
   the last payment. They renew automatically, so they are never on the renewal list, only on the
   lapsed list if the charges have stopped.

3. Comped memberships (board, volunteers, honorary - Payment Method says Comp) don't get either letter.

4. When someone upgrades, the system gives them a new member number and leaves the old record behind.
   Same email means same person; only their newest record counts.

Please save it as renewals.csv, one line per member who gets a letter: member_id, name, email,
list (DUE or LAPSED) and expires - the date the membership ran out or runs out (for sustainers the
paid-through date), written like 2026-10-04.
