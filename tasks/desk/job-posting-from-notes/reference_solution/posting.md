# Front Desk Patient Coordinator - Riverbend Physio Northside

**Location:** 4410 Ridge Rd, Tacoma, WA
**Schedule:** Full-time, Tuesday through Saturday, 11:00 a.m. to 7:30 p.m.
**Pay:** $21.00 to $24.50 per hour, depending on experience

## About the role

You'll be the first person our patients meet at Riverbend Physio Northside. You check patients in and out, answer the phones, schedule and reschedule visits in WebPT, verify insurance benefits before first visits, and collect copays and balances.

## What you bring

- At least 1 year of front desk or reception experience, ideally in healthcare
- Comfort with insurance verification (we train you on our payers)
- CPR/BLS certification within 90 days of hire; we pay for the class

## Benefits

- Medical and dental
- 401(k) with a 3% match
- 10 days PTO plus paid holidays

Email your resume to jobs@riverbendphysio.com by October 12, 2026.
