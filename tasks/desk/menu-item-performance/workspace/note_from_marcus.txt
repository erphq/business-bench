Menu review for August

I want to see every menu item for August with how many we sold, what it brought in, what it cost us, and the margin in dollars - ranked by how many we sold and ranked by margin, so I can see what to push and what to cut.

How to read the POS export:
- Modifiers print as their own lines (the ones starting with +). They are not menu items. What we charge for them and what they cost belong to the item they were added to - an oat milk latte is a latte.
- Sales are what the customer actually paid, the Net Price. Staff meals are half price.
- Comps are on the house: the food still went out, so count the item and its cost, but it earned nothing.
- Voided lines were rung in by mistake and never made. Ignore them completely.
- We renamed Avocado Toast to Smashed Avocado Toast on August 15 - same dish. Report it once under the new name; the cost sheet only has the new name.
