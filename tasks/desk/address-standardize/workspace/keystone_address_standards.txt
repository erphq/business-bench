KEYSTONE MAILING SERVICES - address standards for client lists
(condensed from USPS Publication 28; lists that do not meet this are returned or surcharged)

1. Capital letters. No periods or commas anywhere in the address.
2. Delivery Address is one line: number, pre-directional, street name, suffix,
   post-directional, then the secondary unit. Anything on a second address line
   (apartment, suite, unit) moves to the end of the Delivery Address.
3. Street suffixes use the USPS abbreviation. Accepted spellings we see on client lists:
  ST    Street, St, St., Str
  AVE   Avenue, Ave, Ave., Av
  RD    Road, Rd, Rd.
  DR    Drive, Dr, Dr.
  LN    Lane, Ln, Ln.
  CT    Court, Ct, Ct.
  BLVD  Boulevard, Blvd, Blvd.
  PL    Place, Pl, Pl.
  CIR   Circle, Cir, Cir.
  PKWY  Parkway, Pkwy, Pky
  TER   Terrace, Ter, Terr
  TRL   Trail, Trl
  WAY   Way
4. Directionals: NORTH N, SOUTH S, EAST E, WEST W, NORTHEAST NE, NORTHWEST NW,
   SOUTHEAST SE, SOUTHWEST SW, before or after the street name.
   Exception: when the direction word IS the street name (the word right before the
   suffix, e.g. 45 North Street), keep it spelled out: 45 NORTH ST.
5. Secondary unit designators: APARTMENT APT, SUITE STE, UNIT UNIT, BUILDING BLDG,
   FLOOR FL, ROOM RM. If the list only shows #, write # then a space then the number
   (22 OAK ST # 5).
6. Post office boxes: PO BOX 123.
7. City in capitals. State as the two-letter postal code.
8. ZIP Code: five digits, keeping leading zeros (05401). If the list has the ZIP+4, keep it
   as 12345-6789.

Import file columns, in this order: Customer ID, Full Name, Delivery Address, City, State,
ZIP Code. Full Name in capitals.
