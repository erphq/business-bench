# Counter refund procedure

Granite Peak Outfitters - keep at the register. Follows the posted return policy and the September 2026 changes.

## Before you start

- Refunds to the original payment method are allowed within 30 days of purchase.
- From day 31 to day 60 after purchase, give store credit on a gift card instead of a refund.
- No returns after 60 days, and no returns on Final Sale items.

## Steps

1. **Look up the original sale** in Lightspeed by receipt number or the customer's phone number, and confirm the purchase date.
2. **Inspect the item.** Tags must be on and the item unworn and unused. Put a return tag on it with the receipt number so it goes back to stock or into the damaged bin.
3. **Get approval for refunds over $200.** Text Joshua and wait for a yes before you go on.
4. **Process the refund** to the original payment method. For a cash sale, refund cash from the drawer up to $75; above $75, do not open the drawer - take the customer's mailing address and Gary mails a check within 5 business days.
5. **Email the customer the refund receipt** from Lightspeed.
6. **Log the refund** in the Refunds Log sheet on the shared drive: date, receipt number, amount, reason, and who approved it if approval was needed.
