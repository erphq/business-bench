Riverbend Animal Rescue - Gift reporting policy (board approved, March 2024)

1. Fiscal year. Our fiscal year runs from 1 July to 30 June and is named for the year in which it ends:
   FY2026 is 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026. All board figures are by fiscal year.

2. What counts as raised. Cash gifts count at the amount received: card, check, ACH, PayPal, gifts of stock
   (at the value on the day received) and donor-advised fund grants.

3. In-kind gifts. Goods and services are recorded at fair market value so we can thank the donor, but they
   are not fundraising revenue. Leave them out of totals, donor counts, rankings and retention.

4. Refunds and chargebacks. A gift refunded in full shows Status 'Refunded' and did not happen. A partial refund
   or a card chargeback is entered as its own negative line naming the gift it reverses; net it against that
   original gift, in the original gift's fiscal year, even when the refund is processed later.

5. Donors. A donor is a Donor ID. The CRM prints the name as it was typed on each gift.

6. Retention. Donor retention for a year is the number of donors who gave in the prior fiscal year and gave
   again in this one, divided by the number of donors who gave in the prior fiscal year. 'Gave' means net
   cash giving above zero in that year.
