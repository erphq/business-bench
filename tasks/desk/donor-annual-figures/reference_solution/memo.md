# FY2026 fundraising figures

Net cash raised in FY2026 (1 July 2025 - 30 June 2026) was $207,635.00, against $187,578.00 in FY2025,
a change of $20,057.00. 156 donors gave cash in FY2026.

Donor retention was 62.3%: 94 of the 151 FY2025 donors gave again in FY2026.

The top donor was Thomas Johnson at $20,973.00.

Things to know:

- The $35,000 gift from Halvorsen Foundation was refunded in full, so it is not in FY2026.
- The donated van from Ashgrove Veterinary Partners and the other in-kind gifts are left out of totals, donor counts and retention
  under the gift reporting policy.
- A $4,487 June 2025 card gift was charged back in July; it comes off FY2025, the year of the gift.
