KINJO RAMEN - HOW THE TIP POOL WORKS
(posted by the office, from Hana - updated June 2026)

1. Tips are pooled by shift. Lunch tips go to the people who worked lunch, dinner tips to the people who worked dinner.
   The pool for a shift is the card tips on that shift's closed checks plus that shift's cash envelope. Voided checks
   do not count. We pay card tips out in full and eat the card fee.

2. The POS stamps each check with the time it was closed. Closed before 4:00 PM is lunch; 4:00 PM or later is dinner.
   Checks closed after midnight belong to the dinner shift of the night before.

3. Everyone hourly who worked the shift shares, front and back of house, including shift leads. Managers (anyone whose
   job title says Manager) and I do not take from the pool, even on nights we run food or pour drinks.

4. Your share is by the hours you worked on that shift, clock in to clock out. The Paid hours column on the clock
   export is blank when you clock out after midnight; just count to your clock-out time. If you work lunch straight into
   dinner without clocking out, your time before 4:00 PM counts for lunch and your time from 4:00 PM on counts for dinner.

5. Rounding, so every shift pays out exactly what came in: work each share out exactly, cut it down to the cent, then
   hand out the leftover cents one at a time, first to the person who lost the biggest fraction of a cent. If two people
   lost the same fraction, the one with more hours on the shift goes first, then alphabetical by last name.

6. The payout sheet for payroll: one line per person per shift, with the date, the shift (Lunch or Dinner), the
   employee's name as it is on the time clock, their hours on that shift, and their tip share.
