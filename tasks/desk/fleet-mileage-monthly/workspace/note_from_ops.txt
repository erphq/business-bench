Mileage for the insurance renewal

The carrier wants miles driven per van for each month January through June. The only odometer we have is
what the guys punch in at the pump, so it comes from the fuel card export. Go by the day they fueled, and a
month's miles are the last reading in that month minus the last reading before it.

If a van has no reading at all in a month, do not put 0 - the van was out working, we just don't have a
number. Leave the miles empty and flag it. The next month with a reading then covers everything since
the last one; flag that month too so nobody thinks it was a monster month.

Anything the shop did that touches the odometer is in the shop log.

One line per van per month please: vehicle (like VAN-01), month (2026-01), miles, flag.
