# Wine Club Harvest Dinner

Dear club members,

Please join us for our Wine Club Harvest Dinner on Saturday, October 24, 2026, at the Hilltop Pavilion at Blackthorn Ridge Winery.

The evening begins with a reception on the pavilion terrace at 5:30 PM, and dinner is seated at 6:30 PM. Fennel & Flame Catering is preparing four courses paired with our 2023 Estate Pinot Noir and 2024 Reserve Chardonnay.

## Details

- Where: Hilltop Pavilion, 3711 Ridgecrest Lane, Dundee, OR 97115. Please drive to this address, not the tasting room; the pavilion has its own entrance and parking in the gravel lot beside it.
- Tickets: $95 per member and $115 per guest. Members may bring up to 2 guests. Tickets are charged to the card on file after you RSVP.
- Seating: the dinner is limited to 96 seated guests, so please reply early.
- RSVP by Monday, October 12, 2026 to events@blackthornridge.com or call the tasting room at (503) 555-1480.
- Dress: garden casual. The pavilion is open-sided, so bring a layer for the evening.

We look forward to celebrating the harvest with you.

Dorothy Osei and the Blackthorn Ridge Winery team
