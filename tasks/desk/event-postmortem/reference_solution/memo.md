# Cascade Data Summit 2026 - wrap-up

**Registrations:** 237 registrations stood at the door (9 cancellations were
refunded in full before the event and are excluded from every figure below).

**Attendance:** 192 people scanned in at least once, an attendance rate of 81.0%.
45 registrants never scanned. The scanner logged 412 entries in total, so the raw
scan count is not the headcount.

**Revenue:** gross registration revenue was 124,550.00. 11 refunds were processed in the week
after the event and take 4,635.00 off that, leaving 119,915.00 net.

Three things worth knowing:

1. 11 refunds came through after the event closed, so the registration export overstates revenue by
   4,635.00 until they are netted off.
2. Comp passes are 27 of the registrations and bring in no revenue; only 12 of
   them scanned in, which is the weakest show rate of any ticket type.
3. Student tickets show the second-weakest turnout (22 of 32),
   worth a look before we price that tier again.
