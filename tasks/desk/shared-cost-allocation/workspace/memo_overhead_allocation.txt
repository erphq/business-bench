Re: Monthly overhead allocation - how we do it now

Tomas's July sheet split everything by his own estimates. From August on we allocate on real bases:

1. Building costs go by square feet: rent, property insurance, electric, gas, water & sewer, janitorial and building
   repairs. People costs go by headcount: IT support, software subscriptions, payroll service, the HR & benefits
   platform, phones & internet and office supplies.
2. Square feet: each department's own space from the floor plan. The common break room and restrooms and the space
   we sublet are not a department and stay out of the base.
3. Headcount: employees on the payroll on the last day of the month. Part-time counts as one half. Contractors are not
   employees. Somebody who left during the month, or who has not started yet, does not count.
4. A GL line that already has a department code is a direct cost of that department. It is not overhead and does
   not go into the allocation at all. Credits and refunds net against their account.
5. Rounding: split each account on its own. Work out each department's share, round it to the cent, and put
   whatever difference is left (a cent or two) on the department with the biggest base for that account, so every
   account allocates to the penny.

I want accounts down the side, the five departments across, and department totals, with the formulas left in.
