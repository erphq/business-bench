# Hatch Farm campaign - running notes

_Shirley Chavez, development director_

Fund codes in the database: HATCH = Hatch Farm campaign, GEN = general operating.

## June 3

Kickoff. Board approved a campaign to buy and protect the 212-acre Hatch Farm on Cold Spring Road. Goal: $450,000 (purchase price estimate). Lead gift of $100,000 from the Whitcomb Family Foundation expected this month.

## July 22

The appraisal came in higher than the estimate. The board voted to raise the campaign goal to $525,000 to cover the purchase, closing costs and a stewardship endowment for the land.

## August 30

Good news: the Osei Family Foundation will match new campaign gifts dollar for dollar up to $50,000, through December 31. Formal letter to follow.

## September 8

Fall appeal planning. Raised so far is about $259,000 as of the August 31 board report.

How we count 'raised so far' for anything public: gifts actually received for the HATCH fund (cash, checks, cards, online gifts and stock at its value when received). Pledges don't count until they're paid; each pledge payment counts when it comes in. Returned checks and refunded charges don't count. General fund gifts are not campaign money.

The appeal letter goes to everyone who has given to us before, signed by Priya Harris. It should tell people the goal, how much we've raised so far and how much is left to raise, and explain the match and its deadline so people give in time. Use exact dollar figures from the database as of the latest export, not rounded ones; donors notice when our numbers don't add up.
