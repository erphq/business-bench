Cascade Credit Union wants a lease abstract for every tenant currently in Hawthorn Commons before the refinance closes. All the leases I could find are in the leases folder. Please put them in lease_abstracts.csv, one row per suite, with these columns:

suite - the suite number only (101, not Suite 101)
tenant - the tenant's legal name as written in the lease
commencement_date, expiration_date - YYYY-MM-DD
monthly_base_rent - base rent per month in effect on October 1, 2026, after any increases that have already happened
escalation_type - percent or fixed
escalation_amount - for percent increases the percent as a number (3.5 for 3.5%); for fixed increases the dollar amount monthly rent goes up by each time
renewal_options - how many renewal options the tenant has (0 if none)
notice_deadline - the last day the tenant can give notice to exercise its next renewal option, YYYY-MM-DD, blank if no options
cam_cap_pct - the cap on yearly increases in CAM charges as a percent (4 for 4%), 0 if uncapped
