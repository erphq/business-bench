How we write leadership meeting minutes (Cynthia, updated June 2026)

- Put the date the meeting was held at the top, and who attended (full names).
- Decisions: record what we finally decided. If we changed our minds during the meeting, record where we landed, not the first idea.
  Things we talked about but parked are not decisions.
- Action items: what, who (full name - we have more than one person with some first names), and when, as a calendar date.
- Dates: people say things like 'next Friday' in meetings. In our minutes 'next Friday' means the Friday of the following week,
  not the Friday coming up this week. 'End of the month' is the last day of that month.
- Leadership meets on the first Monday of every month.
