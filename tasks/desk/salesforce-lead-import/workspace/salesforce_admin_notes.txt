Salesforce lead import - notes from Wei Tanaka (our Salesforce consultant), 9 Sept 2026

Use sf_lead_import_template.csv: same columns, same order, one row per lead, drop the Pat Sample row.

LEAD SOURCE is a restricted picklist. Only these values import; anything else fails the row.
  Web                 our website: contact form, quote request form, website chat
  Advertisement       anything we paid to show: LinkedIn ads or sponsored posts, Google Ads / PPC
  Trade Show          booth scans and business cards from any event
  Partner Referral    sent to us by a partner, carrier or reseller
  Employee Referral   sent to us by someone on our own staff
  Phone Inquiry       they called us
  Purchased List      bought or rented lists (ZoomInfo etc.)
  Other               only if none of the above fits

LEAD STATUS (restricted):
  Open - Not Contacted     new, nobody has reached out yet (blank status in the sheet means new)
  Working - Contacted      we have reached out or talked to them, including voicemails
  Closed - Converted       (not used for this import)
  Closed - Not Converted   dead, unqualified or not interested

STATE AND COUNTRY: State and Country/Territory picklists are switched on, so the import takes codes, not names.
Country Code is the two-letter ISO 3166 code (United States US, Canada CA, Mexico MX, United Kingdom GB, Germany DE).
State/Province Code is the two-letter code for US states and Canadian provinces only; leave it blank for every
other country. A blank country in the sheet is a US lead (marketing only leaves it off for US ones).

COMPANY is required on a lead. If there is no company, put [not provided] exactly like that, which is what
web-to-lead does.

LEAD OWNER: the import wizard matches owners on their Salesforce USERNAME, not their email address, and a couple
of ours are different, so use the Username column of the users export. Luis left in August and his user is
deactivated; his leads and any lead without a rep go to Priya Natarajan.
