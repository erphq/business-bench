Ringline contact import - file notes (from your onboarding specialist)

Use ringline_import_template.csv exactly: same six columns, same order, same spelling of the header. One row per
contact in your export, including contacts we will not be able to dial.

contact_id      your CRM id, unchanged.
display_name    First Last.
e164_number     the full international number in E.164: a plus sign, the country calling code, then the national
                number with no spaces, dashes, brackets or trunk zero (so 020 7946 0958 in the UK becomes
                +442079460958 and (02) 5550 1234 in Australia becomes +61255501234). Never include an extension
                here. Leave it blank when import_status is invalid.
extension       digits only, blank when the contact has no extension.
country         two-letter ISO code of the contact's address (US, CA, GB, AU, DE, ...). We use it to pick the
                calling code and to validate the number, so fill it in even when your export left it blank.
import_status   ok when the number is complete and dialable, invalid when it is not (missing digits, extra digits,
                not a phone number at all). A complete national number is 10 digits for US and CA, 10 for GB after
                the leading zero, 9 for AU, and 9 to 11 for DE.

Rows marked invalid still import as name-only contacts, so keep them in the file.
