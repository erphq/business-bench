# First day at Palisade Civil Engineering

_For HR to use in welcome emails. Updated January 2026._

**Office:** 2215 Canal Street, Suite 400, Sacramento, CA 95814. Take the elevator to floor 4 and check in at reception.

**Office hours:** 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

**New hires:** arrive at 8:30 AM on your first day. Orientation with HR runs 8:30 to 10:00, then your manager takes you to your desk.

**Parking:** the Canal Street garage entrance is on 12th Street. Bring your ticket to reception to have it validated on your first day.

**Bring:** original documents for your I-9 - either one List A document (such as a passport) or one List B and one List C document (such as a driver's license and a Social Security card).

**Lunch:** your group takes you to lunch on your first day.
