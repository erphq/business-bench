Operations Coordinator applicants

All the resumes that came in are in the resumes folder (one was a walk-in, I scanned it). Before I start calling people I want candidates.csv, one row per applicant, with:

  email             their email address
  full_name
  phone             10 digits, nothing else
  current_title     the job they're in now; blank if they're not working right now
  current_employer  blank if they're not working right now
  years_experience  years of work experience, one decimal

For experience: count each calendar month someone was working once, including the start and end months (Mar 2019 to Jun 2021 is 28 months). Overlapping jobs don't count twice, gaps between jobs don't count, and school doesn't count. For a job that's still going, count through August 2026. Then divide the months by 12.

If somebody sent us more than one resume, use the most recent one.
