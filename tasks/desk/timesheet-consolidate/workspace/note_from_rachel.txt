Payroll for Aug 30 - Sep 12

All six crew sheets for the pay period are in this folder. I key payroll from one table: one line per person
per payroll week, with employee, week_ending (the Saturday), regular_hours, overtime_hours and total_hours.

- Payroll weeks run Sunday to Saturday. Overtime is anything over 40 hours in a payroll week, counting every
  crew the person worked on that week.
- Pay is time worked less unpaid lunch. Cynthia writes the lunch actually taken in its own column (0 means they worked through).
  Rahul's sheets are start and finish only, so take our standard 30 minute lunch off any shift
  longer than 6 hours. William's hours already have lunch taken off.
- William's sheets run Monday to Sunday because of the weekend irrigation starts. A Sunday on those sheets belongs to
  the payroll week that starts that day; the last Sunday on the second one is next pay period, leave it for then.
