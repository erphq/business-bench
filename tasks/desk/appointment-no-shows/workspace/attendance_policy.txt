Brightpath Learning Center - attendance and cancellation policy (front desk copy)

1. Families must cancel at least 24 hours before the session starts. A cancellation with less than 24
   hours' notice is billed and recorded as a no-show.
2. A cancellation with 24 hours' notice or more takes the session off the book. It is not a no-show and
   it is not a booked session.
3. If a tutor or the center cancels, it is never held against the family, however late.
4. No-show rate = no-shows / sessions on the book (sessions kept + no-shows).
5. The booking app sometimes records the same session twice when a parent taps Book twice. Same student,
   same tutor, same start time is one session.
6. Sessions are reported in the month they were scheduled for.
