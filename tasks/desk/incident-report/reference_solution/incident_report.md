# Silverline Logistics - Incident Report

## 1. Summary
On Thursday 3 September 2026 an order picker, Hiroshi Harris, sustained a laceration and bruising to the right hand at the Line 3 merge of the Tacoma distribution center while freeing a jammed tote. Anna Rivera stopped the line with the e-stop. Hiroshi Harris received first aid on site and was referred to urgent care.

## 2. Date, time and location
- Date: Thursday 3 September 2026
- Time: 14:08 (2:08 PM), the time the Line 3 e-stop was pressed according to the conveyor controller export. Witness estimates ranged from a little after 2:00 to 2:30 PM; the controller log is the record.
- Location: Line 3 merge (device L3-MERGE), pick module, Tacoma distribution center.

## 3. People involved
- Injured person (name, role): Hiroshi Harris, order picker, pick module.
- Witnesses: Anna Rivera (picker at the adjacent station, pressed the e-stop); Cynthia Miller (arrived at the merge after the line had stopped, did not see the moment of injury).
- Supervisor on shift: Dmitri Cook.

## 4. Injury and treatment
Hiroshi Harris sustained a laceration and bruising to the ring and little finger of the right hand, with a possible fracture. First aid was given by Gary Hughes at 14:14: the wound was cleaned and dressed and ice applied. Hiroshi Harris was referred to urgent care (MultiCare Urgent Care Fife) and was driven there by Dmitri Cook at 14:42.

## 5. Sequence of events
1. At 14:07 the controller recorded a jam at the Line 3 merge.
2. Hiroshi Harris reached in to free the stuck tote; the tote moved and the right hand was pinched between the tote and the side rail.
3. At 14:08 Anna Rivera pressed the e-stop at the merge and the line stopped.
4. Anna Rivera walked Hiroshi Harris to the break room, where first aid was given at 14:14.
5. The line was reset at 14:39.

## 6. Immediate actions taken
- Line 3 stopped by e-stop and later reset by the supervisor.
- First aid given and referral to urgent care.
- Facilities taped off the Line 3 merge pending installation of a guard.

## 7. Cause
The cause is under investigation. A root cause review is scheduled for Tuesday 8 September 2026. The witness statement from Cynthia Miller notes that the merge jams several times a week and is cleared by hand with nothing preventing access to the pinch point.

## 8. Corrective actions
| Action | Owner | Due date |
|---|---|---|
| Install a fixed guard at the Line 3 merge pinch point | Mark Torres | 2026-09-17 |
| Jam-clearing refresher (stop the line first) for all pick module staff | Margaret Cox | 2026-09-22 |
| STOP LINE BEFORE CLEARING signs at every merge | Dmitri Cook | 2026-09-08 |
