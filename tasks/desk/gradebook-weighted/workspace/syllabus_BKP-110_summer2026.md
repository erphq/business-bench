# BKP-110 Bookkeeping Fundamentals

Lakeview Adult Learning Center, Summer 2026. Instructor: Dana Okafor.

## Course work

- Six weekly quizzes (10 points each)
- Five homework sets (20 points each)
- Midterm exam (50 points)
- Bookkeeping project (40 points)
- Final exam (100 points)

## How your grade is calculated

| Category | Weight |
|---|---|
| Quizzes | 15% |
| Homework | 25% |
| Midterm | 20% |
| Project | 15% |
| Final Exam | 25% |

Each category is the percentage of points earned in that category.

- Your lowest quiz score is dropped.
- Work that is not turned in scores zero.
- Excused work (documented absence) is not counted for or against you; it is left out of its category.
