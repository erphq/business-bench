Scoring the member survey

Same as last year, please - the 2025 summary is in the folder so you can see the layout. Here is how we
score it:

- Strongly agree is 5, Agree 4, Neither agree nor disagree 3, Disagree 2, Strongly disagree 1.
- Questions 4, 7 and 9 are worded the other way round (agreeing means something is wrong), so score them
  in reverse: Strongly agree is 1 up to Strongly disagree 5. That way a higher average is always better
  and the board can read the whole column the same way.
- N/A isn't a score, and neither is a question someone skipped. Leave them out of both the average and
  the number of responses.
- A few people filled it in twice. Count each member once, using their last submission - the form
  doesn't care about capital letters in the email but they're the same person.
- Our own test runs from foundrycommons.co addresses don't count.

Save it as survey_summary.csv with question (Q1 to Q10), responses and average (two decimals).
