# Keel & Crate Movers - Crew Policies

_Revised March 2025. Effective March 1, 2025._

## 1. Clocking in and out

Crews clock in at the yard on the tablet by the dispatch window and clock out there at the end of the day. Travel time from the yard to the first job and back from the last job is paid time.

## 2. Overtime

Overtime must be approved by the dispatcher before a crew works more than 2 hours past the scheduled end of a job. Call dispatch as soon as it is clear the job will run over.

## 3. Long-distance per diem

Crew members on overnight long-distance jobs receive a per diem of $45 for each night away from home, paid with the next payroll. The company books and pays for lodging directly.

## 4. Tips

Cash and card tips are pooled for each job and split equally among the crew members who worked that job.

## 5. Using your own vehicle

If dispatch asks you to drive your own vehicle to a job site or to pick up supplies, the company reimburses $0.58 per mile. Log the trip on the mileage sheet the same week.

## 6. Damage to customer property

Report any damage to a customer's property or belongings to the dispatcher within 24 hours of finishing the job, with photos.

## 7. Uniforms

Company shirts must be worn on every job. Each crew member is issued three shirts a year and one jacket every two years.

## 8. Safety boots

Steel- or composite-toe boots are required on every job. The company reimburses up to $150 per calendar year with a receipt.

## 9. Phone use while driving

Drivers may not hold or use a phone while the truck is moving. Set navigation before leaving the yard or the job; the crew member riding in the cab handles calls and texts.
