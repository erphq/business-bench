Sales tax on the September orders

Before I invoice the first batch of September orders I need the sales tax on each one. The rate table
from the accountants is in the folder. We only collect in the five states on it; everywhere else is
no tax. Inside a state, use the city row when the ship-to ZIP falls between its From and To (both ends
count), otherwise the 'all other ZIPs' row for that state. The Shipping sheet says whether shipping
gets taxed along with the kits.
