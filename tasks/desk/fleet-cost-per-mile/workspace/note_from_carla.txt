Cost per mile, January to June

For each vehicle I want miles driven, fuel and what we paid the shops, and the cost per mile, plus gallons and MPG so
Dave can see which vans are thirsty. Fleet total at the bottom.

- Miles come from the odometer tab: the 31 Dec reading to the 30 Jun reading. Van 24 went to the dealer in April, and
  Van 30 replaced it; the Fleet tab has the odometer on the bill of sale and the miles Van 30 was delivered with.
- Fuel is the fuel card. Only count the fuel itself - the card also gets used for car washes and the odd coffee and
  snacks, and those are not what it costs to run the van.
- When Luc's crew works the Quebec jobs they fill up over the border and WEX shows those in litres. The dollar amounts
  are already in US dollars.
- Shop cost is the invoice total. Champlain Fleet writes the licence plate instead of the unit number on some invoices.
