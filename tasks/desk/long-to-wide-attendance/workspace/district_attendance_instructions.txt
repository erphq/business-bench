Out-of-School-Time Grant - attendance submission (fall session)
Lakemont Unified School District, Expanded Learning Office

Please return attendance on the attached template, one file per program.

- One row per enrolled student, including students who did not attend. Do not include
  visitors, siblings or trial-day guests. Delete the example row.
- Student Name must be the legal name as it appears on your enrollment roster, written
  Last, First. Do not use nicknames.
- Rows in alphabetical order by Student Name.
- Keep the date columns exactly as they are in the template, one per scheduled session.
- Codes:  P = present   T = tardy   E = excused absence   U = unexcused absence
- Leave a cell blank when you have no record for that student on that day. Do not fill
  blanks with U; we treat a blank as not scheduled or unknown.

Program rules we agreed with Gearhouse:
- Sessions start at 3:30 PM. A check-in after 3:40 PM is T; 3:40 PM or earlier is P.
- Excused and unexcused absences come from the coach's absence sheet (Excused? Y = E, N = U).
- If a student has a Robotics Club check-in on a day the coach also marked them absent, the
  check-in stands (they arrived late after the coach took the register).
