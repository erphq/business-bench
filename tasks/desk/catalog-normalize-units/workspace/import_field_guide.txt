Fieldstone POS - product import field guide

sku        Your item code. Must be unique.
name       Product name as it should print on the receipt.
category   Must match one of your store's categories exactly, including capital letters:
           Beverages | Bulk | Dairy & Eggs | Home & Cleaning | Pantry | Personal Care | Snacks
pack_qty   How many units are sold together as one item. A single item is 1.
unit_size  The size of ONE unit, as a number only.
unit       One of: ml | g | ea  (lower case). Volumes in millilitres, weights in grams.
           For counted goods (tea bags, swabs, eggs) use ea and put the count in one package in unit_size.
price      Selling price as a number, no currency symbol.

Rows that do not match these rules are rejected by the importer.
