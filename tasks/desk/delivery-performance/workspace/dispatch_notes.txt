Reading the stop file (please read this before you start)

Scanned at is what the handheld sends, and the handheld talks UTC. Our hubs do not: in August
Portland runs seven hours behind UTC, Denver six, Austin five. The promised window is what the
customer was told, in their own time.

Windows: AM is 08:00 to 12:00, PM is 12:00 to 17:00, All day is 08:00 to 17:00, and the rest are
written out. A delivery is on time if it is in the window - the day alone is not good enough, that
is the whole argument we keep having with the retailers.

Big items go out on two trucks. Both stops carry the same shipment number. It is one delivery to
the customer and it is only on time if the last piece is in the window.

Attempted, refused and cancelled stops are not deliveries. Leave them out of the percentage.

The drivers type the late reason however they like. We report five reasons - weather, mechanical,
customer, address, route volume - and everything else, including the blanks, is not recorded.
