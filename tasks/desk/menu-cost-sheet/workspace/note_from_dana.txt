Costing the fall menu

The quantities on my cards are what ends up on the plate, not what we buy. Most things go on the
plate the way they come in, but a few lose weight on the prep table first:
  - Salmon side: we lose about 30%
  - Russet potatoes: we lose about 15%
  - Yellow onion: we lose about 10%
  - Parsley: we lose about 35%

So those cost more per plate than the price sheet suggests. Work it from what's left after trim:
a kilo of salmon side only gives us 700 g we can serve, so a gram on the plate costs the kilo
price divided by 700, not the kilo price plus 30%.
