Job margins for the partners' meeting

I want to see, job by job, what we have actually earned against what it has actually cost us, and
I want the ones that are under water flagged so we stop arguing about which they are.

Revenue is what we have invoiced. A draft invoice is not revenue - it is a hope. Anything we wrote
off is not revenue either.

Cost is everything the job has consumed. The hours in the unbilled file are hours we have paid for,
so they are cost at the cost rate on the row, whether or not the client ever sees a bill for them.
Supplier credits come off the cost of the job they were raised against.

Every job on the job list goes on the report, including the ones we have not billed yet.

- Fatima
