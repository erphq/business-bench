# Job margins, first half 2026

Across the nine jobs we invoiced 1,626,584.22 against costs of 1,289,952.54, a margin of
336,631.68. Cedar Point Clinic is the strongest job at 88,775.56.

**Two jobs are losing money.** Umber Ceramics Studio is the worst at -46,716.33 - it has
0.00 of invoiced revenue against 46,716.33 of cost. Quarry Road Nursery is also under
water at -20,564.47; it only turns negative once the hours we have not billed are costed in.

Two things that move these numbers:

1. Revenue here is invoiced work only. Draft and written-off invoices are excluded, which is why
   Umber Ceramics Studio shows the revenue it does.
2. Cost includes 232 unbilled time entries worth
   339,739.00 at cost rate, and is net of the supplier credits raised
   against each job.
