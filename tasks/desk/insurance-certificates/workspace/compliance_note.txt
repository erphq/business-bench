Vendor insurance review - 15 September 2026

The certificates vendors sent us are in the certificates folder. For the compliance log I need coi.csv with one row per insurance policy:

  policy_number    as printed
  insured          the vendor named as the insured
  carrier          the insurance company that wrote the policy (not the agency)
  coverage         general_liability, auto_liability, umbrella or workers_comp
  each_occurrence  general liability and umbrella: each occurrence limit; auto: combined single limit; workers comp: employers' liability each accident
  aggregate        general liability: general aggregate; umbrella: aggregate; 0 for auto and workers comp
  effective_date   YYYY-MM-DD
  expiry_date      YYYY-MM-DD
  status           expired if the policy's expiry date is before 15 September 2026, otherwise active

Limits as plain numbers. If a vendor sent more than one certificate, only log the most recent one.
