From: Nadia Osei

The shop buyers want our fall wholesale list before the trade show, and I would like to send it as a single web page they can open from the email - one file, nothing loaded from anywhere else, and no scripts, because half the buyers' email systems strip them.

It is the wholesale list, so wholesale prices only. Group it by category in this order, because buyers read from the top and holiday is what they are ordering right now:

  1. Holiday
  2. Birthday
  3. Thank You
  4. Sympathy
  5. Everyday
  6. Notebooks
  7. Art Prints

For every item show the price each and the pack price. Cards only go out in boxed packs: the pack price is the price each times the number in the box, less 10% for a box of 6 and 15% for a box of 12. Notebooks and prints never get a pack discount.

Anything discontinued stays off the list - we are out of wedding cards altogether. People have marked discontinued lines in different ways over the years, so look carefully.
