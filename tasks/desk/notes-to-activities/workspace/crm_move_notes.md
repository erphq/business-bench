# Moving the tracker notes into Pipedrive

From Jason, 11 Sept. Pipedrive's activity import is set up with the template in this folder.

- One activity per note. Most Notes cells have several notes in them. Each note starts with the date it
  happened; a line that just carries on from the note above is part of that note, not a new one.
- We started the tracker in November 2025. If a note doesn't say the year, it's November or December 2025
  or January to August 2026. A date later in the text (like an install date) is not a new note.
- Activity Date as YYYY-MM-DD.
- Type is Call, Email or Meeting:
  - Call: called, call, phoned, left vm, left voicemail
  - Email: emailed, email, sent quote
  - Meeting: met, site visit, stopped by, walkthrough
- Owner Email is the Pipedrive user who did it. The initials on the note are the person (JM = Jason Miller
  and so on, however they're typed). No initials means it was the account's rep.
- Maria Bennett (MB) left in January and her login is deactivated; Pipedrive won't take activities for her,
  so everything that would be hers goes under Sofia Cruz, who took over her accounts.
- Note: the text of the note is fine as it is, without the date and initials.
- Accounts with nothing in Notes don't need anything.
