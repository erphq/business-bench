# Q3 2026 wholesale sales

Net wholesale revenue for July to September was $196,009.00 after $2,735.00 of refunds.
Earl Grey Cream was the biggest seller at $50,600.00.

Things to know:

- Import batch B0819-2 on 19 August is a duplicate of batch B0819-1: the portal retried the sync and pushed the same 9 order lines again under new order numbers ($3,230.00). They are left out.
- Yunnan Gold was renamed Golden Yunnan on 10 August and got a new SKU (TL-YG-1 became TL-GY-1). The report shows it as one product, $36,134.00 for the quarter.
- Refunds are taken off the month they were issued, so the 2 July refund on a June order reduces July.
