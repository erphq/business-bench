Expense reports - receipts

Use the template: one row per receipt.

  receipt_no  the receipt, check, folio, trip, ticket or invoice number printed on it (just the number)
  date        the date on the receipt, as YYYY-MM-DD (for the hotel, the checkout date)
  merchant    the business name as printed at the top
  subtotal    before tax
  tax         0 if none is shown separately
  tip         0 if none
  total       what was actually charged to the card, tip included

Amounts as plain numbers. If a receipt shows up twice, report it once - we reimburse each charge one time.
