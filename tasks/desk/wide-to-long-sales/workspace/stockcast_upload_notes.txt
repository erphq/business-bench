Stockcast - sales history upload (from our onboarding call, 2 Sept)

Stockcast already has everything through June 2025 - that went in last summer
(stockcast_upload_2025-06.csv). This upload is July 2025 onwards, straight from the
by-month sales report.

Use the template columns in that order. One row per customer per calendar month.

  Record ID      customer code, a dash, then year and month run together:
                 EMB-1234-202510 for October 2025. Stockcast uses it to update a month
                 instead of adding it twice if we upload again.
  Customer Code  the EMB- code from the report
  Month          YYYY-MM
  Net Sales      plain number, two decimals, no $ or commas. A month where returns beat
                 sales goes in negative.

Only months where the customer actually had sales. Stockcast reads a missing month as zero,
so do not add rows of 0 for the empty cells. No total or subtotal lines - it only wants
customers.
