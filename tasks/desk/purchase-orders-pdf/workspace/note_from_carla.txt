Order entry from customer POs

Everything that came in this week is in customer_pos. The order system imports the template, one row per PO line:

  po_line_ref   the PO number, a hyphen, and the line number exactly as the PO prints it
  customer_id   from customer_list.csv
  po_number     as printed
  po_date       the date on the PO, YYYY-MM-DD
  line          the customer's line number as printed
  part_number   our part number (IF-...)
  description   as printed
  quantity      pieces
  unit_price    price per piece
  line_amount   quantity x unit price
  need_by       when the customer needs that line, YYYY-MM-DD

Customers send changes as a new revision of the same PO number - we build to the latest revision only, and its PO date is the date of that revision. A line that is struck out or marked cancelled is not ordered; leave it out and keep the other line numbers as they are.
