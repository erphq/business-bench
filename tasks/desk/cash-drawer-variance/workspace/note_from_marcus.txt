How closing works here:
- Two drawers: the front desk (haircuts) and the retail counter (products). Both start the day with a $150 float that
  stays in the drawer overnight.
- At close, whoever is on pays the barbers their card tips in cash out of the front drawer. Cash tips go straight into
  the barbers' pockets and never touch a drawer - the POS only records them for payroll.
- Paid outs (towels, coffee and so on) come out of the drawer during the day and the POS already knows about them.
- Then both drawers' cash, less the floats, goes in ONE bag, and the bag goes in the night drop at Summit the next
  morning we are open. We are closed Mondays, so Sunday's bag goes in on Tuesday.
- Card sales settle to the account by themselves, so ignore those.
- Once in a while the credit union counts a bag differently and posts an adjustment a few days later that names the
  deposit. That is still that bag.
