GRANT TERMS CHEAT SHEET - keep with the tracking file
(from the award letters; Nia, Development)

State Clean Water Fund (SCWF) - award period 1/1/2026 to 12/31/2026
  - Costs are allowable only if incurred inside the award period. Use the invoice date, not the date we posted it.
  - No food or beverages of any kind, including volunteer events.

Hartwell Family Foundation (HFF) - award period 2/1/2026 to 1/31/2027
  - Same rule on the award period.
  - The foundation does not pay staff mileage or travel, even when it is booked to a staff line.

County Parks Community Grant (CPCG) - award period 3/1/2026 to 10/31/2026
  - Same rule on the award period.
  - Food for volunteers is fine. Gift cards and alcohol are never allowable.

Split-funded costs
  - The budget line column shows the split, e.g. 'SCWF-PER 60% / CPCG-STI 40%'. Work out each grant's share of the
    expense: every grant but the last one listed is rounded to the cent, and the last one listed gets whatever is left,
    so the pieces add back to the expense exactly. Each piece is then allowable or not under its own grant's rules.

Anything disallowed comes off the grant and gets paid from unrestricted funds. Credits and refunds reduce the line they
were booked to. GEN-OPS lines are ordinary operating costs that were never charged to a grant, so they are not grant
spending and not disallowed costs either - leave them out.

For the board packet: for each grant and budget line, the budget, what we have spent that the grant will actually pay
for, and what is left, plus what we disallowed. Formulas please, the auditors like to trace them.
