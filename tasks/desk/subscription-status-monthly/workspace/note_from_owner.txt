Subscriber counts - how I count them

A subscriber is someone paying us for coffee. The free first bag is a trial: they are not a subscriber
until their first paid box, and if they cancel during the trial that is not a lost subscriber either.

Active in a month means they have a paid period that covers the last day of that month. Quarterly
people pay once for three months, so they are active all three.

When someone cancels, they keep getting coffee until the end of the period they already paid for.
Count the cancellation in the month that paid period ends, not the month they emailed us.

If somebody cancels and comes back later, they are active again from their new paid period, and the
earlier cancellation still counts in its month.

The billing tool retries a failed card the next day, so a failed invoice usually has a paid twin.

I want January to June: active subscribers at the end of each month and cancellations in each month.
