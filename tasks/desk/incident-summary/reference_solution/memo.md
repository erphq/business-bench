# Safety summary, January to June 2026

111 incidents were recorded across the four sites, alongside 68 near misses reported on the
floor tablets. Near misses are counted separately and are not in the incident figure.

**Plant 1 had the most incidents this half, 46 of them**, ahead of Plant 2 on
41. Lacerations are the single biggest category at Plant 1 with 22 of them, which is where the
next toolbox talk should go.

The Warehouse recorded no injuries at all this half, but it did log 24 near misses, so it stays
on the report rather than dropping off it.

Two notes on the data: 5 rows in the log are marked voided or reported
in error and are excluded, and the 3 late Q1 entries repeated at the top of the Q2 sheet are counted
once.
