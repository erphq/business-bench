Nordwerk 2026 prices into our catalog

Nordwerk's 2026 dealer price list came in. It's in dollars but everything else is metric, and our
catalog is inches and pounds and priced by the piece. Last year's file is in the folder so you can see
the layout. Please make catalog_us.csv with sku (their Art.-Nr.), description, length_in, width_in,
weight_lb, pack_qty and price_each.

- 1 inch is exactly 25.4 mm and 1 pound is exactly 453.59237 g. Watch the units - they write mm, cm and
  m, and g and kg, and not always the same way in one row.
- Our rounding: lengths and widths to the nearest sixteenth of an inch, written as a decimal (17.6875,
  not 17 11/16). Weights to three decimals of a pound. Prices to three decimals, since some hardware
  sells for pennies apiece.
- The weight on their list is already per piece.
- price_each is what one piece costs. Their 'Price per' column says what the price is for: one piece, a
  whole PU (pack), or 100 or 1000 pieces. pack_qty is the PU.
- Leave out anything they've discontinued.
