How payments are applied
  1. Payments are due on the 1st of each month. A payment received by the 10th is on time.
  2. Interest is paid in arrears. Each monthly payment pays the interest for the calendar month before its
     due date, at the rate in effect for that month.
  3. That interest is the unpaid principal balance after the previous payment, times the annual rate,
     divided by 12, rounded to the nearest cent. The rest of the payment reduces principal.
  4. Additional principal payments are applied entirely to principal on the day they are received. They
     do not change the monthly payment or the next due date.
  5. When the rate adjusts, the monthly payment is recalculated and the new amount is due from the
     payment after the adjustment date.
