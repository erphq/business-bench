Cohort table - notes for whoever builds it (Leila)

A customer's cohort is the month of their first ever order with us. The web shop opened in July 2025, so between the
two exports in this folder you have the complete order history. Anyone who bought in 2025 is an existing customer,
not a new one, and does not belong in a 2026 cohort even if they ordered again this year.

An order that was cancelled, or refunded in full, never happened as far as this table is concerned. A partial refund
is still a real order.

I want to see, for each month January to June, how many new customers we got, and then how many of those same people
ordered again one month later, two months later, and so on. A person counts once in a month no matter how many orders
they placed. Where a later month has not happened yet, leave the cell blank or mark it n/a.
