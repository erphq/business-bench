# Journal entries (JNL)

**JNL-1.1** Every manual entry carries its support: attach the source document (or a copy of it) to the entry
before submitting or posting it.

**JNL-1.2** An entry of $5,000.00 or more needs the controller's approval before it is posted. Submit it for
approval; post it once it is approved. The person who prepares an entry never approves it.

**JNL-1.3** An entry below $5,000.00 is posted by its preparer, with its support attached.

**JNL-1.4** An entry the controller returns is corrected, supported and resubmitted; it is not posted as it was.
