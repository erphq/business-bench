# Northgate Valve Co. handbook

Accounting close and system access. Clause numbers are referred to in the ERP, in audits and in performance
reviews, so cite them when you explain an entry.

- [Month-end close](close.md) (CLS)
- [Journal entries](journals.md) (JNL)
- [System access](access.md) (SEC)

The staff accountant prepares the close entries; the controller reviews and approves entries that need approval
and closes the period once the entries are posted and the control accounts tie.
