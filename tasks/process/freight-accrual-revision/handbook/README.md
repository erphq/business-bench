# Northgate Valve Co. handbook

Accounting: inbound freight accruals and journal entries. Clause numbers are referred to in the ERP, in audits
and in performance reviews, so cite them when you explain an entry.

- [Inbound freight accruals](freight.md) (FRT)
- [Journal entries](journals.md) (JNL)

The staff accountant books the month-end accruals; the controller reviews them and closes the month once they
are right.
