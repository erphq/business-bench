# Northgate Valve Co. handbook

Company policies for vendor bank details, payment runs, and cash. Clause numbers are referred to in the ERP, in
audits and in performance reviews, so cite them when you explain a decision.

- [Vendor master data](vendors.md) (VEN)
- [Payments](payments.md) (PAY)
- [Treasury](treasury.md) (TRE)

Who does what: AP clerks record requests from vendors; the AP supervisor verifies bank-detail changes and prepares
the payment run; the controller approves and releases the run; the bank reports cleared payments the next
business day.
