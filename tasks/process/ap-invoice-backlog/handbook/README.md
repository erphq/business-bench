# Northgate Valve Co. handbook

Company policies for accounts payable. Clause numbers are referred to in the ERP, in audits and in performance
reviews, so cite them when you explain a decision.

- [Accounts payable](payables.md) (AP)
- [Vendors paid without a purchase order](vendors.md) (VEN)

Who does what: buyers raise purchase orders and agree prices with vendors; the warehouse receives deliveries against
purchase orders; the AP clerk and the AP supervisor enter and match vendor invoices; the AP supervisor or the
controller releases holds; the controller approves invoices the AP supervisor entered for payment. Maya Chen,
operations manager, is the buyer of record for stock items and approves price changes. Department heads approve
spending for their departments.
