# Northgate Valve Co. handbook

Company policies for purchasing, receiving and accounts payable. Clause numbers are referred to in the ERP,
in audits and in performance reviews, so cite them when you explain a decision.

- [Purchasing](purchasing.md) (PUR)
- [Receiving](receiving.md) (REC)
- [Accounts payable](payables.md) (AP)

Who does what: department heads approve requisitions up to their limit and forward larger ones; buyers turn
approved requisitions into purchase orders; the warehouse and buyers receive; AP clerks enter and match vendor
invoices; the AP supervisor or the controller releases holds and approves invoices for payment.
