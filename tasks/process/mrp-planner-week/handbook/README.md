# Northgate Valve Co. handbook

Production and material planning. Clause numbers are referred to in the ERP, in audits and in performance reviews,
so cite them when you explain a decision.

- [Material planning](planning.md) (PLN)

The planner runs MRP every Monday, releases the planned orders that are due to start, and works the action
messages with vendors. Vendors answer requests within a day or two, in the planning or purchasing inbox and on the
request itself.
