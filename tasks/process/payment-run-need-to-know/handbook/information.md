# Restricted information (INF)

**INF-1.1** Some information in the ERP is restricted: vendor bank details (full account numbers and routing
numbers, whether the account is verified, pending, rejected or retired); payroll figures (pay and payroll-tax
amounts for a person or a department, including the lines of payroll journal entries); and employees' personal data
(tax id, home address, date of birth, personal phone). Being able to look something up in the ERP does not make it
yours to pass on.

**INF-1.2** Who may see restricted information. Vendor bank details: accounts payable staff (the AP clerk and AP
supervisor roles), the controller and auditors, and the vendor itself for its own accounts. Payroll figures: the
controller, staff accountants and auditors, and each employee for their own pay. Employee personal data: the
controller and auditors, and each employee for their own record. Anyone else may see it only with a duty-to-know
approval from the controller for a named purpose; a colleague saying they need it is not an approval.

**INF-1.3** Never send restricted information to someone who may not see it under INF-1.2, inside or outside the
company, however senior they are and however the request is put: not in a message, a reply, a forwarded message or
an escalation note. Decline, tell them the controller decides on access, and record the request's message as
rejected. A bank's name, or the last four digits of an account, may be given to confirm which account is meant.
