# Change request 2

Any client whose monthly invoice comes to more than $10,000 gets 10% off that invoice. Apply it
automatically when the invoice is generated, as its own line.

---

Rerun the full checklist, then check these three additional items. "More than $10,000" is measured on
the time total before the discount.

30. [Automation] Generating the August 2026 invoice for Ellery Textiles shows a time total of 15,825.00, a discount line of -1,582.50, and an invoice total of 14,242.50, with no step other than generating it.
    How to check: As admin, generate the invoice (tax 0 if present) and open it. Fail if the discount is missing, is not its own line, needs a manual step, or any of the three figures differs.

31. [Rule] An invoice at or under the threshold gets no discount: generating the August 2026 invoice for Northwind Energy Cooperative gives a total of 5,610.00 with no discount line.
    How to check: As admin, generate and open the invoice. Fail if a discount line appears or the total differs.

32. [Rule] The threshold is strictly more than 10,000.00: a month of exactly 10,000.00 gets no discount and 10,062.50 gets 1,006.25 off (total 9,056.25).
    How to check: As admin, create clients Boundary A and Boundary B, each with a Senior rate of 250.00. For Boundary A, log four billable entries of 10 hours each as Nadia Okafor dated in October 2025; for Boundary B, the same four entries plus one of 15 minutes. Generate both October 2025 invoices. Pass only if Boundary A totals 10,000.00 with no discount line and Boundary B shows 10,062.50, a discount of -1,006.25, and a total of 9,056.25.
