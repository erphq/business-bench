# Change request 3

Rename the job stages to Scheduled / En route / On site / Done / Invoiced. And stop making
invoices under $50, it's not worth the paperwork: roll those into the next invoice for that
customer, and let me see what's waiting to be billed. Once a job has been invoiced it should
show as Invoiced.

---

Rerun the full checklist, reading "closing a job" in checklist.md as moving it to **Done**.
Then check these three additional items. "Under $50" means a billable total below 50.00; a
job totaling exactly 50.00 is invoiced normally.

38. [Exact] The job stage field offers exactly Scheduled, En route, On site, Done, Invoiced, in that order, and nothing else; every existing job shows one of the five.
    How to check: As admin, open a job's stage selector and list its options; then scan the jobs list. Fail on any extra, missing, or differently spelled option, or any job showing an old stage name.

39. [Rule] A job that reaches Done with a billable total under 50.00 does not create an invoice; it stays at Done and its amount is visible as waiting to be billed.
    How to check: As admin, for one customer X create job J1 with 1 x CAP-45-5-440 (38.50), 0 labor hours, a technician assigned; move it to Done; reload. Pass only if no invoice exists for J1, J1's stage is Done (not Invoiced), and 38.50 appears as held or uninvoiced on the job, the customer, or a held/pending list. Fail if an invoice for 38.50 is created.

40. [Automation] The held amount rolls into that customer's next invoice, and both jobs become Invoiced.
    How to check: Immediately after item 39, create job J2 for the same customer X with 1 x MTR-COND-14HP (289.00), 0 labor hours, a technician assigned; move it to Done; reload. Pass only if exactly one new invoice exists for X, its total is **327.50** (289.00 + 38.50), it shows lines from both J1 and J2, and J1 and J2 both show stage Invoiced. Fail if the invoice is 289.00, if two invoices were created, or if J1 is still at Done.
