Don't let a request be fully approved if it would take its budget line below zero. The department head
can still sign off on their step; the final approval is what gets blocked, and no PO number gets used.

Rerun the full checklist, then check these three additional items. They use the Transportation line
**01-3600-5300** (Dues & Memberships), which has **1,234.50** remaining; delete each test request once
its item is read.
