# Change request 3

Quotes of $10,000 or more need our shop foreman to confirm the lead time before they go out. Give
the foreman a login that shows quotes and lead times but never prices, costs, or margins.

---

Rerun the full checklist, then check these three additional items. As admin, invite
`tester.foreman@example.com` with the foreman role and open the link in a private window.

33. [Rule] A quote totaling 10,000.00 or more cannot be sent until the foreman confirms its lead time; a quote under 10,000.00 sends directly: 2 × SHK-DT96 in WAL-84-FAS at 5,000.00 (10,000.00) is held, and 2 × SHK-DT96 in WAL-84-FAS at 4,995.00 (9,990.00) sends.
    How to check: As restricted, create "Shop test" (10,000.00) and try to send (expect a refusal or a waiting-for-shop state, not Sent). Create "Shop test 2" (9,990.00) and send (expect Sent). Fail if Shop test is sent or Shop test 2 is held.

34. [Permission] The foreman confirms the lead time and can do nothing else to the quote: the foreman login can record the lead-time confirmation on "Shop test", after which restricted can send it; the foreman cannot change a line's quantity or price, or send the quote.
    How to check: As the foreman, confirm the lead time on Shop test (expect saved), then try to change a line quantity and to send the quote (expect no control, or a rejection and no change after reload). As restricted, send Shop test (expect Sent). Fail on any other outcome.

35. [Permission] The foreman never sees money: no unit price, discount, total, cost, or margin appears for the foreman on screen or in data responses.
    How to check: As the foreman, open the quotes list, Shop test, and any order screen reachable; then in devtools > Network inspect the responses that loaded them. Pass only if no price, discount, total, cost, or margin value is shown and no populated field of that kind (such as unit_price, total, cost, margin) is in any response. Fail on a single occurrence. If the app is server-rendered with no data responses, only the on-screen check applies.
