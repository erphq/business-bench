# Change request 3

Add a Compliance stage between Review and Approved: our compliance officer has to sign off there
before I can approve, and she should see every vendor but not be able to change anything except
that sign-off.

---

Rerun the full checklist, reading "approve" in items 20 to 23 as recording the Compliance sign-off
and then approving. Then check these three additional items.

33. [Exact] The vendor stage field offers exactly New, Documents, Review, Compliance, Approved, Rejected, in that order, and nothing else; no existing vendor shows a blank or unknown stage, and the dashboard's Approved vendors figure is unchanged by the new stage.
    How to check: As admin, note Approved vendors on the dashboard, open a vendor's stage selector and list its options, scan the vendors list, and reload the dashboard. Fail on any extra, missing, or reordered option, any vendor without a valid stage, or a changed Approved vendors figure.

34. [Rule] A vendor at Review cannot go straight to Approved, and a vendor at Compliance cannot be approved before the sign-off is recorded.
    How to check: As admin, create a Packaging vendor, move it to Documents, and record a W-9, a Certificate of Insurance (expiry 2028-06-30), and a Code of Conduct so it reaches Review (set Review by hand if it does not move on its own; item 21 already tests that). Try to set Approved (expect a refusal or no option). Move it to Compliance and try to approve before any sign-off (expect a refusal). Fail if either approval succeeds.

35. [Permission] A compliance login sees every vendor and can only record the sign-off: its vendors total equals the admin's, across all five categories; it can sign off the item-34 vendor; it cannot edit that vendor's phone number or set it to Approved; afterwards the admin can approve it.
    How to check: As admin, invite `tester.compliance@example.com` with the compliance role and open the link in a private window. As that user, compare the vendors total with the admin's, sign off the item-34 vendor (expect saved), then try to change its phone number and to set Approved (expect no control, or a rejection and no change after reload). As admin, approve it (expect success). Fail on any other outcome.
